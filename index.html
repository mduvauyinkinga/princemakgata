The Designer

A place to be — friendly, welcoming, easy to talk to.

Prince Makgata is a South African fashion designer, stylist, master of ceremonies and social media influencer — the creative force behind Prince Makgata The Designer (PMd), headquartered in Mpumalanga. From the first sketch to the final stitch, every garment that leaves the atelier is cut for one person: you. Suits that command the boardroom. Traditional wear that carries heritage. Alterations so precise they look untouched.

Located at Shop No 1, Mimosa Centre, Botha Ave — right on point, you'll never get lost.
